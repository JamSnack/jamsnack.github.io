I have decided to start a Devblog for my current game project "BASEWORLD!"

BASEWORLD! is gonna be a 2D sandbox game and is inspired by Terraria and Dig or Die. You'll be exploring procedurally generated worlds, fighting various bosses, looking for epic loot and resources, and upgrading your character and your base. The big catch is that no world is permanent - the Archfiends are seeking to destroy yours one Wave of Creeps at a time. The only way to survive is to build a base and take it to the next world!

Right now I am in a pre-alpha state. I'm working on getting multiplayer set up so that I can finish the singleplayer gameplay loop. Things are getting pretty cool, so stick around to find out what happens next!
